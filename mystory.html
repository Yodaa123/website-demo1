My story

A little about the person behind tuesday co, Robert tuesday.

It all started for me when I was a little kid, I used to make little short films on my old PS3. It went from that to making videos with a laptop and Windows Movie Maker. After a while, I was put into a video editing class in high school, and I fell in love with it.

I can do video editing, lighting, graphic design, and a little web development. Whether you want someone's help to edit your video, someone to shoot an amazing music video for you, or something else, I’m here to help!

I started tuesday co. because I want to help like-minded individuals and businesses bring their ideas to life in whatever way I can. I mostly do all this for the love of the craft, and I’m passionate about creating meaningful work, not just making a profit.
